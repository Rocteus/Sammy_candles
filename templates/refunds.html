Our Guarantee

Refund & Return Policy

We want you to adore your Sammy_candles experience. Here is how we ensure your complete satisfaction.

1. Damaged or Defective Items

If your candle arrives damaged, shattered, or defective during transit, we will gladly replace it or issue a full refund. Please send a photo of the damaged item to our support team within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Unopened & Unburned Returns

Changed your mind about a scent? Unopened, unburned candles in their original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery for store credit (minus shipping costs). Customers are responsible for return shipping fees.

3. Burned & Final Sale Items

Because fragrance preference is subjective and our candles are poured in small batches, burned or lit candles are final sale and cannot be returned for cash refunds.

4. Questions?

Reach out to us via our Contact Page and we'll be happy to help you find a scent you love!

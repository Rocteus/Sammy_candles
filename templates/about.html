Our Clean, Handcrafted Philosophy

Pure ingredients. Zero toxins. Exceptional ambiance.

We believe that filling your home with beautiful fragrance should never come at the expense of your health or the environment. Traditional mass-market candles often use paraffin wax—a petroleum byproduct—and synthetic additives that release harmful pollutants when burned.

That is why every single candle we offer is meticulously hand-poured in small batches using 100% organic coconut-soy wax, unbleached cotton wicks, and phthalate-free fragrance oils.
